# 16. DATA QUALITY & RESEARCH INTEGRITY

These requirements are mandatory.

### No Fabricated Information

Never invent:

- Company revenue
- AdSense earnings
- Monthly traffic
- Employee numbers
- Sponsorship contracts
- Advertising rates
- Acquisition values
- Historical financial performance

### Data Classification

Every important data point must be classified as:

A. Verified – official audited or disclosed data.

B. Disclosed – publicly stated by the company but not necessarily audited.

C. Third-party estimate – estimated by a recognized external platform.

D. Analyst estimate – calculated using documented assumptions.

E. Unknown – no reliable information found.

### Historical Data Rules

- Do not fill missing years with fabricated figures.
- Do not interpolate missing financial data without explicitly labeling it.
- Do not mix parent-company and subsidiary financial figures.
- Do not compare different financial reporting periods without explaining the difference.
- Do not confuse gross revenue with net revenue.
- Do not confuse advertising revenue with AdSense revenue.
- Do not confuse website traffic with monetized pageviews.

When a company does not publish financial statements, explain the limitation.
