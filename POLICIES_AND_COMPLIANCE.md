## 2. Bangladesh legal & regulatory context (verify locally)

> The following are **general orientation points**, not exhaustive or current legal advice. Confirm with a Bangladeshi lawyer / CA.

- **Company registration:** Registrar of Joint Stock Companies and Firms (RJSC) — private limited company is typical. Requires name approval, Memorandum & Articles, directors/shareholders.
- **Tax:** National Board of Revenue (NBR) — TIN required; VAT registration if turnover exceeds threshold; corporate income tax on profits; possible Minimum Tax.
- **Tobacco/alcohol/betting restrictions:** advertising of prohibited goods/services is restricted — do not accept such advertisers.
- **Employment:** Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 (as amended) — contracts, working hours, leave, termination, provident fund where applicable.
- **Copyright:** Copyright Act 2000 (as amended) — protect original content; do not infringe; honour takedowns. Sports broadcasts are protected; restreaming without rights infringes.
- **Data protection:** Bangladesh has been developing data-protection legislation; align with international best practice (consent, minimisation, security, breach handling) and any enacted law.
- **Television/online broadcasting:** any licensed broadcasting/streaming of events requires rights from rights holders; unlicensed restreaming is unlawful.
- **Advertising standards:** self-regulatory norms + consumer protection law — no misleading claims.
- **Foreign investment / repatriation:** if foreign investors are involved, Bangladesh Bank regulations on inward/outward remittance and shareholding apply.

## 3. International considerations

- **GDPR (EU)** if you have EU users: lawful basis, consent, data-subject rights, DPA.
- **CCPA/CPRA (California)** if applicable.
- **Google AdSense Program Policies** — invalid traffic, prohibited content, placement rules; suspension risk.
- **DMCA / takedown** processes for copyright.
- **FTC/ASA-style** advertising disclosure norms (sponsored/affiliate).

## 4. Mandatory vs recommended (quick view)

- **Do first (legal):** register company, TIN/VAT, founder & shareholder agreements, employment contracts, privacy/cookie/terms, copyright & broadcasting-rights discipline, AdSense compliance.
- **Build early (recommended):** editorial independence, verification/fact-check, sponsorship/affiliate disclosure, financial & expense policies, risk & continuity.
- **Mature later:** crisis management, investor communication, formal exit policy.
