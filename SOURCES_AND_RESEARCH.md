## 3. Bangladesh & South Asia market context (estimates/analysis)
- Mobile-first, price-sensitive audience → ad-supported (AdSense) fits earlier than hard paywalls.
- Cricket and football dominate; football (EPL/La Liga/BPL-era interest) and cricket (internationals/BPL) are anchors.
- Rising digital ad spend and creator economy; reachable local sponsors (telecom, beverage, sportswear, banks).
- Broadcasting rights are expensive and licensed — never restream without rights.
- Page RPM is geography-sensitive: South Asian traffic typically lower RPM than US/EU; traffic mix matters.
