Business Overview

What We Are Building

A digital sports media company producing news, journalism, analysis, video, live coverage, podcasts, newsletters and community across 15+ sports, with a Bangladesh & South Asia core and international reach.

🎯 Mission

Deliver fast, verified, independent sports journalism and analysis that South Asian and global fans trust — monetised responsibly across advertising, sponsorship, memberships and (legally licensed) streaming.

👁️ Vision

Become the most credible independent sports news & data brand for Bangladesh and the wider South Asian region, expanding into video and live products.

⚖️ Values

Editorial independence · Accuracy & verification · Legal & copyright compliance · Transparency with audience and sponsors · Sustainable, diversified revenue.

Bangladesh & South Asia Context Estimate

  • Cricket + football dominate attention; football (Bangladesh Premier League-era interest, EPL/La Liga following) and cricket (BPL, internationals) are anchors.
  • Mobile-first, price-sensitive audience → ad-supported (AdSense) fits better than hard paywalls early.
  • Rising digital ad spend and creator economy; local sponsors (telecom, beverage, sportswear, banks) are reachable.
  • Broadcasting rights are expensive/licensed — never restream without rights.
  • RMB (revenue per mille) is geo-sensitive: South Asian traffic typically lower RPM than US/EU; mix matters.

Realistic Takeaways for a Small Independent

  • Win a niche (e.g., Bangladesh football + local cricket) before going broad.
  • Use SEO + Google Discover + social for low-cost reach.
  • AdSense first, then layer direct ads & sponsorships.
  • Differentiate on verification & independence, not volume.
  • Build owned audience (newsletter/community) to reduce platform dependency.
  • Only pursue streaming with licensed or owned content.

A. Google AdSense Display Advertising Primary (initial)

How it works: display ad units (in-article, multiplex, auto ads) serve across desktop & mobile; video ad eligibility requires video content & approval. The company earns on a per-impression / per-click basis mediated by Google. Revenue ≈ Pageviews ÷ 1000 × Page RPM. Not every visit yields an impression and not all pageviews monetise equally (geography, device, season, ad blindness).

Infrastructure: CMS, ad slots, consent/CMP, analytics.
Initial inv.: low (mostly dev time).
Monthly OpEx: hosting/CDN.
Difficulty: low–medium (approval + policy).
Revenue calc: PV/1000 × PageRPM; PageRPM = f(geo, CTR, CPC, fill).
Margin: high (90%+ gross).
Scalability: high (traffic-bound).
Time to rev.: 3–6 mo after approval.
Pros: easy start, passive.
Cons: low RPM in region, volatile.
Risks: invalid traffic, policy bans.
Partnerships: Google only.
⛔
Policy & compliance: AdSense prohibits invalid traffic, prohibited content, deceptive placements, and certain content categories. Account suspension risk is real. Maintain a compliance policy, monitor for invalid traffic, and never buy bot traffic. See POLICIES_AND_COMPLIANCE.md.

B. Direct Advertising

Sell placements directly to brands: banner ads, sponsored articles, sponsored match previews/analysis, sponsored video, newsletter/podcast/social sponsorships, brand campaigns, sponsored sections. Priced via rate card (flat monthly, CPM, or package). Higher margin than AdSense because no network cut.

Infra: ad server or manual slots, CRM, media kit.
Inv.: low–medium.
OpEx: ad manager time.
Difficulty: medium (sales).
Revenue: price × placements.
Margin: 70–90%.
Scalability: medium (sales capacity).
Time: 4–8 mo.
Pros: high value, relationships.
Cons: lumpy, sales effort.
Risks: advertiser churn.
Partnerships: brands, agencies.

C. Paid Sponsorship

Tiered packages (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum/Exclusive) for sports brands, equipment/apparel, tech, telecom, consumer/beverage, event organizers, academies, local businesses. Deliverables include logo placement, sponsored content, event coverage, social, reporting.

D. Sports Blogging & Journalism

Monetise via search traffic, Google Discover, evergreen content, exclusives, expert/tactical analysis, match reports, investigative pieces, historical content, opinion. Revenue is indirect (feeds AdSense + direct + subscriptions). SEO is the growth engine.

SEO strategy: topical authority, structured data, Core Web Vitals, Discover optimisation, internal linking.
Editorial cost: writers + editors.
Production: per-article cost.
Revenue: traffic → ads; exclusives → subs.
Margin: high at scale.
Scalability: high (content moat).
Time: 3–12 mo (SEO lag).
Risks: Google dependency, fake-news/credibility, correction rate.
Mitigation: verification & corrections policy.

E. Live Streaming Business Rights-Sensitive

⛔
Never assume publicly available broadcasts can be legally restreamed. Only stream (a) owned programs, (b) properly licensed/authorized events, or (c) commentary/shows around events you have rights to. Broadcasting rights & copyright are strictly enforced.

Formats: owned shows, authorized live sports, live commentary, pre/post-match shows, interviews, press conferences, discussions, tournament coverage (licensed), exclusive streaming partnerships. Revenue: streaming ads, video sponsorship, subscription, PPV, premium membership, sponsored shows, brand integrations, licensing/distribution.

Infra: encoder, CDN, player, DRM, moderation.
Inv.: medium–high.
OpEx: CDN/bandwidth (scales with viewers).
Difficulty: high.
Revenue: viewers/1000 × stream RPM + subs.
Margin: medium (bandwidth).
Scalability: high but cost scales.
Time: 12–24 mo.
Risks: rights claims, takedowns, CDN cost spikes.
Partnerships: rights holders, leagues.
