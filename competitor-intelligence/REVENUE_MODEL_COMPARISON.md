# Revenue Model Comparison

> Observable business-model presence only. **Not** financial magnitude. No fabricated figures.

## Models tracked
Display ads · Direct ads · Programmatic · Subscription · Sponsorship · Live streaming · Broadcasting rights · Affiliate · Licensing · Events.

## How to read
- **●** = observable model presence (from public/observable info).
- **—** = not observed / Unknown.
- Presence ≠ magnitude. A company may use a model at small scale.

## Patterns (observable)
| Type | Typical model mix |
|------|-------------------|
| Digital-only sports | Display + programmatic + direct (+ sponsorship) |
| Subscription sports | Subscription (+ limited ads) |
| Sports broadcaster | Rights + subscription + ads (+ PPV) |
| General news w/ sports | Display + circulation + subscriptions |
| Public-service | License fee / state funding + limited ads |
