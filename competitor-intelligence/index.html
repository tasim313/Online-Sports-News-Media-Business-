Key Learnings (qualitative, sourced)

  • Ad-supported free models (Cricbuzz, Goal, Sportskeeda) scale on SEO + social; subscription-first (The Athletic, NYT) monetises depth.
  • Broadcasters (ESPN, Sky, DAZN) monetise rights + subscriptions; capital-intensive.
  • Public-service (BBC) differs (license fee); state-backed (Al Jazeera, France 24) differ again.
  • AdSense is rarely the whole story at scale — direct sales, programmatic (Ad Manager), sponsorships and subscriptions layer on top.
  • Parent vs brand distinction is critical (Cricbuzz→Times Internet; The Athletic→NYT).

Success & Failure Analysis

Lessons

✅ Success patterns

  • Owned audience (newsletter/community) reduces platform risk
  • Diversified revenue (ads + subs + sponsors)
  • Strong SEO + brand trust
  • Disciplined costs vs revenue

⚠️ Failure patterns

  • Over-reliance on one platform (Google/Facebook)
  • AdSense-only with low RPM
  • Costs outpacing revenue
  • Rights/copyright missteps
