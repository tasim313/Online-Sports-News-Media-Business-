# Bangladesh Market Opportunities

> For a new Bangladesh-based sports news & media company. Qualitative, sourced/observable. No fabricated market sizes.

## Study closely
- **Cricbuzz** — scores + SEO + regional dominance (brand; parent Times Internet).
- **Prothom Alo / The Daily Star** — local trust, Bangla/English reach.
- **ESPNcricinfo** — data & stats moat.

## Avoid head-on (capital/scale barriers)
- Broadcasting rights (ESPN, Sky, DAZN) — capital-intensive.
- Global football scale (Goal, OneFootball) — hard to out-scale.

## Niche opportunities
- Bangladesh **local & grassroots** sports, academies, school/college sport.
- **Women's sport** coverage (underserved).
- **Bangla-language** tactical analysis & data explainers.
- Regional South Asia sports business.

## Language & geography
- **Bangla-first** for local depth; **English** for international reach.
- Bangladesh core → South Asia → international.

## Content formats to prioritise
- News + match previews/reports + evergreen explainers + data pieces.
- SEO + Google Discover + social + newsletter (owned audience).

## Revenue sequence
1. AdSense (after approval) + affiliate.
2. Direct advertising + sponsorship (local telecom/beverage/sportswear).
3. Memberships / paid newsletter.
4. Licensed streaming (only with rights).

## First 10,000 visitors
SEO (topical authority) + Google Discover + social distribution + newsletter capture.

## First advertisers
Local brands via a simple media kit (reach, demographics, packages Bronze→Exclusive).

## Moat
Trust & verification, owned audience, local depth, data.
