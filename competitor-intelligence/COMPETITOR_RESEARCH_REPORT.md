## 3. Key qualitative findings (sourced/observable)
- **Ad-supported free** (Cricbuzz, Goal, Sportskeeda, 90min) scale via SEO + social; AdSense is rarely the whole story at scale — direct sales, programmatic (Ad Manager/AdX), sponsorships layer on top.
- **Subscription-first** (The Athletic, NYT) monetise depth; The Athletic acquired by NYT (2022).
- **Broadcasters** (ESPN, Sky, DAZN, CBS, NBC) monetise rights + subscriptions + ads; capital-intensive.
- **Public-service / state-backed** (BBC license fee; Al Jazeera, France 24 state funding) differ structurally.
- **Parent vs brand** matters: Cricbuzz→Times Internet; The Athletic→NYT; Goal→Footballco/DAZN group.
- **AdSense ≠ Google ad tech.** Header bidding / Ad Manager / AdX are distinct from AdSense.
