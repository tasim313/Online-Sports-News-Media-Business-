# Research Sources

**Research date:** November 2026. Verify all figures at source before use. No private data accessed.

## Financial sources (for sourced entry)
- Company annual reports & audited accounts.
- SEC filings (e.g., NYT Company — NYSE: NYT).
- UK Companies House filings.
- Stock-exchange disclosures.
- Parent-company reports (Times Internet, Warner Bros. Discovery, Comcast, Disney, Paramount, RCS, PRISA, Amaury, Godó).
- Credit-rating reports.

## Business sources
- Official company websites, investor relations, media kits.
- Press releases, acquisition/partnership announcements.
- Company presentations.

## Traffic & marketing (estimates)
- Similarweb, Semrush, Ahrefs, Google Trends, Google News, Google Search.
- These are **third-party estimates**, not company disclosures.

## Industry sources
- Reuters Institute (Digital News Report).
- WAN-IFRA, IAB, WARC, Deloitte, PwC.
- Credible sports-media trade research.

## Citation format (per data point)
Source name · URL · publication date · reporting period · access date · data class (Confirmed/Disclosed/Estimate/Unknown) · reliability.

## Note
This dataset stores **no numeric financials**; figures must be entered from the above sources and classified. Blank = Unknown.
